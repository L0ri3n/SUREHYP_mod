================================================================================
WHY THIS FIXES SAM CLASSIFICATION
================================================================================

SAM (Spectral Angle Mapper) computes angles between spectra in n-D space.

Before Fixes:
  ❌ Extreme spikes (65,000) → Distorted angles
  ❌ Water vapor bands → False matches in absorption regions
  ❌ Discontinuities → Inconsistent spectral shapes
  ❌ Result: Misclassifications, errors, poor accuracy

After Fixes:
  ✅ Normal reflectance range → Correct angles
  ✅ Water vapor masked → No false matches
  ✅ Smooth spectra → Consistent shapes
  ✅ Result: Accurate classification, better discrimination
